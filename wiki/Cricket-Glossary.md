# Cricket Glossary (Beginner Edition)

Everything you need to know about cricket terms used in wt20-oracle. **No cricket background needed!**

---

## Basic Match Structure

| Term | Simple Explanation | Example |
|------|------------------|---------|
| **T20** | Super fast cricket match (20 overs per team) | Most exciting cricket format |
| **Over** | 6 balls thrown by one bowler | An "over" takes ~1 minute |
| **Inning** | One team's turn to bat | India's inning vs Australia's inning |
| **Match** | One complete T20 game | Takes 2-2.5 hours total |
| **Wicket** | A batter getting "out" (eliminated) | Getting 10 wickets wins the game |
| **Runs** | Points scored | Hitting ball to boundary = 4 runs, over boundary = 6 runs |

### Example Match Timeline
```
India's turn to bat (20 overs = 120 balls):
  Over 1-6:    PowerPlay (aggressive phase)
  Over 7-16:   Middle overs (strategic phase)
  Over 17-20:  Death overs (final push)
  
Result: India scores 145 runs, loses 6 wickets

Australia's turn to bat (20 overs):
  Need to score 146+ runs to win
  (Same timeline as India)
```

## Batting Terms

| Term | What It Means | Example |
|------|--------------|---------|
| **Batter** | Player trying to hit the ball | Smriti Mandhana, Alyssa Healy |
| **Strike Rate (SR)** | How fast you score (runs per 100 balls) | SR of 125 = you score 125 runs for every 100 balls |
| **Average** | Average runs per game | If you scored 100, 50, 75 in 3 games, average = 75 |
| **Dot Ball** | Ball with 0 runs (no score) | Hit the ball but it goes to a fielder |
| **Boundary** | Hitting ball to the edge (4 runs) | Ball rolls to the boundary line |
| **Six** | Hitting ball over the boundary (6 runs) | Ball goes over the boundary without bouncing |
| **Out / Wicket** | Batter is eliminated | Caught by fielder, bowled (ball hits stumps), LBW, etc. |
| **Not Out (NO)** | Still batting when innings ends | Didn't get out |

### Batter Roles

| Position | Role | Example Player |
|----------|------|-----------------|
| **#1, #2 (Openers)** | Start the game (first to bat) | Fast, aggressive |
| **#3** | Captain's role (middle order stabilizer) | Balanced: can hit and defend |
| **#4-6** | Middle order (flexible batters) | Adapt to match situation |
| **#7-11** | Lower order (mostly bowlers who bat) | Can hit in emergency |

### Understanding Strike Rate

```
Strike Rate Ranges:
  < 100   = Slow (conservative, lots of dot balls)
  100-120 = Medium (steady scoring)
  120-140 = Fast (aggressive)
  > 140   = Very fast (very aggressive)

Example:
  Smriti Mandhana's SR = 125
  Means: In 100 balls faced, she scores 125 runs
  Or: About 1.25 runs per ball on average
```

## Bowling Terms

| Term | What It Means | Example |
|------|--------------|---------|
| **Bowler** | Player throwing the ball (like pitcher) | Jhulan Goswami, Renuka Singh |
| **Bowling** | Throwing the ball (cricket term, not actual bowling) | "Renuka is bowling" = she's throwing |
| **Economy Rate** | Runs given per over | 6.2 economy = bowler gives 6.2 runs per over |
| **Wicket (bowler)** | Getting a batter out | "Took 2 wickets" = got 2 batters out |
| **Pace Bowler** | Fast bowler (120+ km/h) | Jhulan Goswami (very fast) |
| **Spin Bowler** | Bowler using rotation (slower) | Ravindra (leg-spin), Axar (off-spin) |
| **Yorker** | Ball at the base of stumps (hard to hit) | Specialist bowlers use in death overs |
| **Death Bowler** | Expert in final overs (17-20) | Needs accuracy under pressure |

### Understanding Economy Rate

```
Economy Ranges:
  < 6.0   = Tight (hard to score against)
  6.0-7.0 = Good (standard)
  7.0-8.0 = Loose (batters scoring well)
  > 8.0   = Very loose (very hittable)

Example:
  Jhulan's economy = 6.8
  Means: For every 6-ball over, she gives away 6.8 runs
  So: Average of 1.13 runs per ball
```

## Head-to-Head (Matchups)

| Term | Meaning | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| **Matchup** | History between one batter and one bowler | Smriti vs Megan Schutt |
| **vs Pace** | Record against fast bowlers | Smriti scores 125 SR vs pace |
| **vs Spin** | Record against spinners | Smriti scores 118 SR vs spin |
| **Reliability** | How confident we are in the stat | "High" = many meetings, "Low" = few meetings |

### Example Matchup

```
Smriti Mandhana vs Megan Schutt:

Overall: 25 meetings
  Smriti scored: 125 runs off 100 balls
  Strike Rate: 125

vs Megan specifically:
  Meeting 1: 12 runs off 8 balls
  Meeting 2: 18 runs off 11 balls
  Meeting 3: 20 runs off 12 balls
  etc.

Conclusion: Smriti does VERY WELL against Megan (125 SR)
Recommendation: Back Smriti to score when Megan bowls
```

## Game Phases

### PowerPlay (Overs 1-6)
- **Duration:** First 6 overs (36 balls)
- **Strategy:** Aggressive (try to score quickly)
- **Typical Strike Rate:** 130-150
- **Example:** Shafali Verma opens and plays aggressive shots

### Middle Overs (Overs 7-16)
- **Duration:** Overs 7 through 16 (60 balls, 10 overs)
- **Strategy:** Build score steadily, balance risk & safety
- **Typical Strike Rate:** 110-130
- **Example:** Harmanpreet Kaur rotating strike, building

### Death Overs (Overs 17-20)
- **Duration:** Final 4 overs (24 balls)
- **Strategy:** Aggressive hitting to finish strong
- **Typical Strike Rate:** 160+
- **Example:** Richa Ghosh hitting sixes to finish

```
Score Building Timeline:
  PowerPlay (6 overs):    Expect 40-50 runs (aggressive)
  Middle (10 overs):      Expect 70-90 runs (steady)
  Death (4 overs):        Expect 20-35 runs (finishing)
                          ─────────────────────────────
  Total (20 overs):       Expected total: 130-175 runs
```

## Examples to Understand Better

### Example 1: Strike Rate
```
Smriti faces 100 balls and scores:
  Run 1: Hit 1 run
  Run 2: Hit 4 runs (boundary)
  Run 3: 0 runs (dot ball)
  Run 4: Hit 2 runs
  Run 5: Hit 6 runs (six)
  ...continuing for 100 balls total...
  Total: 125 runs off 100 balls

Strike Rate = (125 ÷ 100) × 100 = 125

Meaning: For every 100 balls, she scores 125 runs
         Or: About 1.25 runs per ball
```

### Example 2: Economy Rate
```
Jhulan bowls 1 over (6 balls):
  Ball 1: 0 runs (dot)
  Ball 2: 2 runs
  Ball 3: 0 runs (dot)
  Ball 4: 1 run
  Ball 5: 2 runs
  Ball 6: 2 runs
  Total: 7 runs in 6 balls

Economy = 7 runs per over (above average)
Meaning: Batters are scoring well against her
```

### Example 3: Matchup
```
Smriti vs Megan Schutt (all time):

Meeting 1 (Jan 2023):
  Smriti scored 15 runs off 12 balls

Meeting 2 (Mar 2023):
  Smriti scored 18 runs off 14 balls

Meeting 3 (Oct 2023):
  Smriti scored 20 runs off 10 balls

Pattern: Smriti always scores well vs Megan

Conclusion: 
  Overall SR = 53 runs ÷ 36 balls = 147 SR
  Recommendation: Smriti should face Megan!
```
